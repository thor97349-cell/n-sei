BRIEFING

You are the attacker. Infiltrate the NODE-7 data center, hack the terminal in the DATA ROOM (hold E), carry the data module out and hold an extraction zone: the WEST LOADING DOCK or the EAST GARAGE.

Three defenders guard the facility. They hear you: running, gunfire, doors and breaching all make noise. Walk (Shift) or crouch to stay quiet. Wooden barricades can be shot down to open new routes.

The mouse is captured while you play. Press Esc at any time to release it and pause.
